Why Sleepfactor

We bring together habit logging and sleep tracking so you can see what really makes a difference to your rest, based on your own routine.

How it works

Three simple steps from daily habits to insights you can use.

01

Log your habits

Track the things you do each day that might affect sleep, like caffeine, exercise, supplements, and more. Quick logging so it fits your routine.

02

Sync your sleep data

Connect Apple Health or Google Health Connect so your sleep stays in one place, private to you.

Connects with Apple Health & Google Health Connect.

03

See what impacts you

See how your habits link to sleep duration, stages, and quality. The Correlation Engine shows how strong each link is, so you know what to trust.
